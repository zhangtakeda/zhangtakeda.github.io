I am a Ph.D. student in plasma physics at Dalian University of Technology, working on numerical algorithms and scientific computing for fusion plasma equilibrium, transport, and integrated modeling. My research involves the development of numerical methods and simulation codes in Python and C++, with particular interests in numerical optimization and high-performance computing. I am advised by Prof. Zhengxiong Wang and Prof. Feng Wang, and collaborate with Dr. Huasheng Xie at Beijing VeloAlpha Technology Co., Ltd. on numerical algorithm development and scientific software implementation.
